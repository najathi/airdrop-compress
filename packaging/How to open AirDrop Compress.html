Opening AirDrop Compress

This free app isn't notarised by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the first time you open it. It takes about a minute, and you only do it once.

On macOS 15 Sequoia and later Most Macs

1

Drag the app to Applications, then open it

macOS shows a warning. Click Done (not Move to Trash).

⚠️
“AirDrop Compress” Not Opened
Apple could not verify “AirDrop Compress” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.
DoneMove to Trash
2

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security

Scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to “AirDrop Compress” was blocked.

Privacy & Security
Security
“AirDrop Compress” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway
3

Confirm with Open Anyway

Click Open Anyway again, then enter your Mac password or use Touch ID.

⚠️
Open “AirDrop Compress”?
macOS cannot verify the developer of “AirDrop Compress”. Are you sure you want to open it?
Open AnywayMove to Trash
4

Allow access to your AirDrop folder

AirDrop Compress needs to read the folder AirDrop saves to. Click Allow.

📁
“AirDrop Compress” would like to access files in your Downloads folder.
AirDrop Compress watches your Downloads folder, where AirDrop saves received files.
Don't AllowAllow

Prefer Terminal? Any macOS

After dragging the app to Applications, paste this into Terminal and press Return. Then open the app normally.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/AirDrop Compress.app"

This removes the “downloaded from the internet” flag from this one app only. Only do this for apps you trust.

On macOS 12–14 (Monterey, Ventura, Sonoma)

In Applications, right-click (or Control-click) AirDrop Compress, choose Open, then click Open in the dialog.

✓ That's it. Look for the AirDrop Compress icon in your menu bar. New AirDrop files are converted automatically.
